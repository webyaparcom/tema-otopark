FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Common questions and short answers.

How is the fee calculated?

By parking time on an hourly, daily or monthly tariff; contact us for current rates.

Can I reserve a space?

Yes; you can reserve in advance through the form or by phone.

Are the images and data on the site real?

No; this is a setup example. Images are representative and names, figures and people are fictional.

Is there liability for damage or loss to my car?

Terms are set out in the parking agreement; please review it at entry. We advise not leaving valuables in the car.
